# Mehngai Meter 🇵🇰

> **Pakistan @79 — see how far your rupee has fallen.**

Mehngai Meter is a Pakistan-focused inflation calculator that shows what money from 1947–2025 is worth in 2026 PKR. It turns abstract inflation into relatable everyday comparisons—such as naan, chai, petrol, gold, salary, and cars—through a simple, mobile-friendly experience built around Pakistan @79.

Inflation in Pakistan is often discussed through complicated percentages, but people struggle to understand its real impact on purchasing power. Mehngai Meter makes that impact visible: it helps students, families, and young professionals see how much the rupee has lost value over time and how the cost of daily life has changed across generations.
